The problem

A forgotten appointment is an empty chair, and that time cannot be sold twice. Calling every patient to confirm takes hours your team does not have.

The solution

Each booking triggers a confirmation, followed by reminders at the times you choose. Patients reply to confirm or reschedule, and anyone who has not confirmed is flagged to your front desk while there is still time to fill the slot.

What it does

  • Sends booking confirmations automatically
  • Sends reminders at the times you choose
  • Patients confirm or reschedule with one reply
  • Flags unconfirmed appointments to your team
  • Updates appointment status in the calendar
  • Keeps messages short and free of treatment details

What it looks like

Workflow

  1. Appointment is booked
  2. Confirmation goes out
  3. Reminder is sent before the visit
  4. Patient replies to confirm or reschedule
  5. Unconfirmed visits are flagged to your team

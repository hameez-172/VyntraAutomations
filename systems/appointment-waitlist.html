The problem

A cancellation leaves a hole in the schedule. Working down a list of waiting patients takes time you rarely have, so the slot is often still empty when the day arrives.

The solution

Patients join the waitlist with their preferred days and times. When a cancellation opens a slot, the system texts up to three matching patients in order. The first YES gets the slot, the calendar updates, your front desk is told, and everyone else is let down politely and kept on the list.

What it does

  • Captures waitlist requests with preferred times
  • Detects cancellations from your calendar
  • Texts up to three matching patients in order
  • Gives the slot to the first YES, after checking it is still open
  • Updates the calendar and confirms by text
  • Notifies your front desk and keeps the others on the list

Workflow

  1. A patient cancels and a slot opens
  2. Matching waitlist patients are texted
  3. The first YES is checked against the open slot
  4. Calendar is booked and the patient confirmed
  5. Others are told it is taken and stay on the list

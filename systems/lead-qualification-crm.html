The problem

Inquiries arrive from forms, calls and chat, then end up in inboxes and notebooks. Nobody can say which ones were answered, which are worth the most or who owns the next step.

The solution

New leads are captured from your website forms, the AI gathers the missing details and qualifies each inquiry, the right team member is assigned, and the CRM record stays current as the lead moves toward a booking.

What it does

  • Captures leads from your website forms
  • Collects the details your team needs
  • Qualifies each inquiry
  • Assigns it to the right team member
  • Updates the CRM automatically
  • Tracks status from inquiry to booking

The problem

Someone reads about Invisalign or implants at 9 PM, has one question and leaves. Questions sent through the site wait until your front team has a free minute, and by then the patient has often booked elsewhere.

The solution

A chat assistant on your website that explains your services in plain, non-clinical language, answers the common questions, collects contact details and helps the visitor choose a time. Anything complex goes to your team with the whole conversation attached.

What it does

  • Answers patient questions around the clock
  • Explains treatments in plain language
  • Collects contact details
  • Qualifies each inquiry
  • Helps visitors choose a time
  • Hands complex questions to your team with the full chat

Workflow

  1. Visitor asks a question on your site
  2. AI answers from your practice information
  3. Contact details are collected
  4. Inquiry is qualified
  5. Visitor is booked or handed to your team

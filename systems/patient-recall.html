The problem

Existing patients are the easiest to keep, yet most practices only reach the ones someone has time to call. A patient who is overdue for hygiene rarely cancels. They just stop coming.

The solution

Each morning the system finds patients who are due, writes a short personal message and reaches out by text and email. When they reply, it books the visit, honours an opt-out straight away, or alerts your front desk if a person is needed. You can see who is due, who was contacted and who booked.

What it does

  • Finds patients who are due for a checkup or cleaning
  • Personal text and email outreach
  • Books the visit when the patient replies
  • Honours opt-outs immediately
  • Alerts your front desk when a reply needs a person
  • Shows who is due, contacted and booked

Workflow

  1. Patients due for recall are identified
  2. Outreach goes out by text and email
  3. Patient replies and picks a time
  4. Visit is booked in your calendar
  5. Contacted, booked and opted-out statuses are tracked

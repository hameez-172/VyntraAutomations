The problem

The dentist recommends a crown, the patient says they will think about it, and the plan sits in the chart. Nobody has time to chase every unscheduled treatment, and the patient often feels awkward calling back.

The solution

Every morning the system finds patients with recommended treatment and no booking, then sends a friendly follow-up on the schedule you set. When they reply, AI reads what they want: it offers open times, books the one they pick, answers simple questions or hands the conversation to your team. Patients who stay quiet become a task.

What it does

  • Finds patients with unscheduled recommended treatment
  • Sends a follow-up on your schedule
  • Tells apart a booking, a question and a request for a person
  • Offers free times from your calendar and books the visit
  • Answers simple questions about the next step
  • Creates a task for your team when there is no reply

Workflow

  1. Treatment is recommended but not booked
  2. Follow-up goes out on your schedule
  3. Patient replies and AI reads the intent
  4. Visit is booked or the question answered
  5. No reply, or a complex one, becomes a staff task

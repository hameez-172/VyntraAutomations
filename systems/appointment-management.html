The problem

Every booking means calls and texts back and forth, and someone retypes the details into the calendar. Now and then a slot is double-booked or a change never makes it in.

The solution

Patients request a time online. The system checks your calendar, books the visit if the slot is free, sends the confirmation and logs it. If the slot is taken, the patient hears straight away. Every morning, tomorrow’s patients get a reminder.

What it does

  • Online appointment requests
  • Live availability check against your calendar
  • Automatic booking confirmation
  • Instant reply when a slot is already taken
  • Every booking logged to a sheet
  • Daily reminders for tomorrow’s patients

Workflow

  1. Patient submits a request
  2. Availability is checked against your calendar
  3. Event is created and the booking is logged
  4. Confirmation goes to the patient
  5. Reminder goes out the day before
